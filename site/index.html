How ordering works

We only take orders here. You pay us directly, never on this website.

Before you order

  1. Pick your bagels. Add flavours and boxes from the menu. Mix flavours in a box by listing them in the notes.
  2. Choose pickup or delivery. Delivery needs your address. Delivery within [your area]. Fee confirmed on WhatsApp.
  3. Pick a day and time. Order by 6pm for next-morning bagels.
  4. Send on WhatsApp. Your order opens in a ready-to-send message. Press send in WhatsApp.

After you send

  1. Wait for our reply. Your order is only confirmed when we answer in WhatsApp, with the final total.
  2. Pay us directly. We tell you how to pay in the chat, at pickup or on delivery. This site never takes money.
  3. Collect or receive. Come to us at Add street address, Nairobi, or watch for our delivery message.
  4. Tell us how it was. Leave a review below. We read every one.

Quick answers

How do I order from Susan's Bagel Bakery?

Add bagels on this page, press Review order, enter your name and when you want them, then send. WhatsApp opens with your whole order already written out. Your order is confirmed once we reply.

Do you take payment on the website?

No. This site only sends your order to us. Payment is arranged directly with us on WhatsApp, at pickup or on delivery.

Do you deliver?

Delivery within [your area]. Fee confirmed on WhatsApp.

How much notice do you need?

Order by 6pm for next-morning bagels.

Where are you and when are you open?

Add street address, Nairobi. Open Mon to Sat, 7am to 5pm.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Message us in the same WhatsApp chat as soon as you can. Changes are easiest before baking starts, so earlier is better.
