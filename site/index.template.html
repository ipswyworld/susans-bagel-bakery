How ordering works

We only take orders here. You pay us directly, never on this website.

Before you order

  1. Pick your bagels. Add flavours and boxes from the menu. Mix flavours in a box by listing them in the notes.
  2. Choose pickup or delivery. Delivery needs your address. {{delivery_info}}
  3. Pick a day and time. {{lead_time}}
  4. Send on WhatsApp. Your order opens in a ready-to-send message. Press send in WhatsApp.

After you send

  1. Wait for our reply. Your order is only confirmed when we answer in WhatsApp, with the final total.
  2. Pay us directly. We tell you how to pay in the chat, at pickup or on delivery. This site never takes money.
  3. Collect or receive. Come to us at {{address}}, or watch for our delivery message.
  4. Tell us how it was. Leave a review below. We read every one.
